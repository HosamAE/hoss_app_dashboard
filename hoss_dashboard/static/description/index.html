Your Odoo. Your Look.

Hoss Dashboard replaces the fixed, one-size-fits-all Odoo Home Menu with one every user controls — background, colors, spacing, icons, greeting, even a live clock — changed in seconds, live, with zero code and zero XML.

🎨 The idea in one line: it's not a theme, it's a canvas — you decide what it looks like.

One click opens the customizer. Everything updates live.

Click the paint-brush icon in the top bar and a full visual panel slides in — no page reload, no waiting. Every change previews instantly on the real Home Menu behind it.

  • 🖌️ Background: solid color, gradient, or your own image
  • 🔲 App icon size & shape — rounded, circular or square
  • 🪟 Glass card opacity, border, radius and color
  • ✍️ Greeting title, subtitle, alignment and font size — even a rich HTML message
  • 🕐 An optional live clock, fully styled to match the theme
